How EcoBite works

Three simple steps toward a world with less food waste.

🍱

1. List surplus food

Restaurants share available food, quantities, locations, and pickup deadlines.

🤝

2. Reserve a donation

A volunteer or recipient can coordinate a pickup using the listed details.

💚

3. Make an impact

Track completed donations and estimate the meals rescued from going to waste.

🌍

Small actions. A greener tomorrow.

Every meal shared is an opportunity to reduce waste and support our community.

*Estimated rescued weight uses 0.4 kg per donated meal. This is an illustrative estimate, not a measured weight.

EcoBite is a demonstration prototype. Listings are saved in this browser only and are not shared between users. Food safety and actual handover must be verified in practice.
